FT TRANSCENDENCE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

FT Transcendence ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our web application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you register or use our platform:

  • Email address and username (required for account creation)
  • Display name, country, gender, and date of birth (optional profile fields)
  • Game history and statistics (wins, losses, match records)
  • Profile avatar images (if uploaded)
  • OAuth provider identifiers (if you use social login)

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Provide and operate the FT Transcendence game platform
  • Display your profile and statistics to other users
  • Enable multiplayer features and friend systems
  • Show leaderboard rankings based on game performance
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account

4. Data Storage

All data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted within a Docker containerized environment. Passwords are hashed and salted using industry-standard cryptographic methods. We do not store plaintext passwords.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Your username and game statistics may be visible to other registered users of the platform as part of the game's social features.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting the platform administrators.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your personal information, including HTTPS encryption for all communications, JWT-based authentication, and hashed password storage.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for optional data fields at any time

9. Contact

This project was created as part of the 42 school curriculum. For any privacy-related questions, please contact the project team through the 42 school platform.
